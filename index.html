Who we are

Nuntiuz is a service in development for configuring business assistants, connecting communication channels and tools, maintaining document knowledge, and reviewing customer conversations. It includes a workspace, a testing playground, WhatsApp connections and an optional website chat widget.

Matias Luna, based in Argentina, operates Nuntiuz. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to that operator. Contact us at lighttextco@gmail.com.

This policy covers Nuntiuz account holders, authorized testers, people messaging a business through a connected channel, and visitors to this policy website. A business using Nuntiuz also determines how it uses its customers’ information and should provide its own privacy notice. We handle that business’s customer conversations on its instructions; we are responsible for our own account administration, security and support activities. Contact either the business or us if you are unsure who should handle a request.

Information we handle

Workspace and assistant information
Account email, password hash, workspace identifiers, assistant names, saved instructions and their versions, selected models, channel settings, and tool configuration. Passwords are processed for registration and login; the database stores a password hash.
Conversations
Messages you send, generated replies, display names, phone numbers or other channel identifiers, conversation identifiers, timestamps, and whether automated replies are active or paused. When a supported channel accepts media, we may process its captions, URLs or media references and related information. Content can contain personal information you choose to include.
Knowledge documents
Uploaded PDF, TXT and DOCX content is processed to extract text. We store file names, sizes and upload dates, extracted text fragments, and numerical representations of that text used for retrieval. Relevant fragments may be used to answer a question.
Connected accounts
Provider account identifiers and labels, permissions you grant, encrypted access and refresh credentials where issued, and authorization expiry information. WhatsApp setup also handles business-account and phone-number identifiers and a temporary two-step verification PIN used to register a number.
Technical and support information
Request and service logs, channel and tool identifiers, operation timing, delivery notifications, errors, website origin, and IP addresses or derived identifiers used for abuse prevention. We also receive the information you include in support or privacy emails. Infrastructure providers may maintain their own technical logs.

Information comes from you, the business operating an assistant, connected messaging or account providers, and technical requests to the service. Provide only information needed for your request. The development service is not intended for collecting sensitive identification, financial, health or other highly sensitive records.

How we use information

  • Create accounts, authenticate users, and manage their assistants and connected channels.
  • Receive messages, maintain conversation context, generate replies, and show conversation history to the relevant business.
  • Retrieve information from uploaded knowledge and perform tools enabled for an assistant.
  • Complete requested account connections, renew credentials where supported, and manage messaging resources.
  • Test and maintain the development service, investigate problems, prevent abuse, answer support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations.

We process information needed to provide the service you request or to carry out the relevant service relationship where the law permits it. We obtain consent where required, including for optional access to connected accounts. Businesses using Nuntiuz must establish an appropriate lawful basis for processing their customers’ information. Publishing this policy, visiting a page, or sending a message does not by itself provide consent for unrelated uses.

Required account and channel information is needed for those features to function. Optional integrations can remain disconnected. Automated replies are generated by AI and may be inaccurate; contact the relevant business for human assistance. Pausing automated replies does not stop messages from being received and stored.

AI processing and knowledge retrieval

Nuntiuz sends the current message, relevant conversation history, assistant instructions and, when used, tool results to OpenRouter. OpenRouter routes requests to the model and provider configured for the assistant. Those services receive the content needed for inference.

Knowledge uploads and search queries also use embedding models through OpenRouter. When knowledge search is enabled, a relevance model may receive the question and candidate document fragments before selected information is returned to the assistant.

We do not run a model-training pipeline in the current Nuntiuz application. However, external providers have their own retention and training practices, which depend on the selected provider, its terms and account settings. We do not promise that every model has zero retention or that every provider excludes training. Ask the operator which providers are enabled before sending confidential information.

Service providers receive information to deliver these features; conversation content is not automatically made public by Nuntiuz. A workspace operator can view the conversations for its own assistants.

Messaging and connected services

Meta and WhatsApp

When a business connects WhatsApp, Meta’s signup flow handles account selection and phone verification. Nuntiuz receives authorization and the business-account and phone identifiers needed to check ownership, subscribe to notifications, register the number and operate the channel. Meta receives outbound message content and recipient identifiers; Nuntiuz receives incoming message content and associated channel information. Meta also processes information under its WhatsApp privacy policy.

The registration PIN is used during the temporary signup attempt and is not retained in the saved account after registration. Disconnecting an account prevents its connected channels from using that authorization and removes the locally saved connection credentials. It does not delete existing messages or remove copies held by Meta.

Optional account and tool connections

If you connect Google, we receive the account identity and authorized credentials. The built-in Google Calendar integration currently lists calendar metadata using a read-only calendar-list permission. If invoked by an assistant, the tool’s results can be included in the model context. Google handles its own services under its privacy policy.

Other enabled tools can connect to Cloudflare, DeepWiki or custom Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers selected by the workspace operator. Invoking them may send a query, tool arguments and conversation-related metadata, including identifiers, to that server. Tool output may be sent back to the AI provider. The data and actions available depend on the tools and permissions enabled for that assistant. These connections are optional; third-party services have their own privacy terms.

Operators and service providers supporting hosting, email delivery, messaging, AI inference or an enabled integration can receive information for those activities. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to address a valid legal request or protect legal rights.

Storage and security

Nuntiuz runs on a hosted server managed by its operator. Application records and knowledge indexes are stored in that server environment. Messaging, AI and enabled integration providers also process information as described in this policy.

The application stores account, configuration and conversation records in a relational database and knowledge fragments and embeddings in a search index. Short-lived authorization attempts and abuse-prevention state are stored separately. These stores are part of the Nuntiuz deployment; using a database product does not itself mean its software vendor receives the stored data.

Workspace access requires authentication. Account passwords are hashed and saved integration credentials use application-level encryption. These measures do not mean that all stored conversation content is encrypted, that only a message’s sender can read it, or that a security certification has been obtained. Security also depends on the server, configuration and enabled providers.

The workspace uses browser local storage for its sign-in token, workspace identifier, account email and interface preferences. Website chat uses session storage for a visitor conversation token; starting a new chat or clearing browser storage does not erase earlier conversations from the server. Optional account connections use temporary cookies to bind authorization to the browser. The current Nuntiuz interface does not include an advertising tracker.

How long information stays

The development application does not automatically expire persistent account records, conversations or indexed knowledge after a fixed number of days. They remain stored while needed to provide and test the service, until an operator removes them or a verified deletion request is handled, subject to applicable legal requirements. We remove information when it is no longer necessary for its purpose.

Temporary authorization attempts and rate-limit records have technical expiry periods. Visitor chat tokens currently expire after 24 hours. Those expiry periods limit authorization or temporary state; they are not conversation-deletion schedules.

Deleting a knowledge document through its document controls removes its indexed fragments and metadata. Removing an assistant or channel from the interface can only mark it unavailable; it does not necessarily erase its stored records. To request complete removal of personal information, follow the data deletion instructions.

Backups or infrastructure snapshots, where used, may contain earlier copies. We assess them as part of a deletion request and explain any lawful retention and removal arrangements. Copies held independently by a messaging service, another recipient or an AI provider are governed by that party’s own obligations and controls.

Your choices and rights

You can request information about your data, access, correction, updating or deletion by emailing lighttextco@gmail.com. You can also request withdrawal of consent or exercise other rights available under applicable law, including objection, restriction or portability where those rights apply. We may verify identity and clarify which account, business or conversation your request concerns.

Under Argentina’s Law No. 25,326, where applicable, access requests must be answered within 10 calendar days, and correction, updating or deletion must be addressed within five business days of receipt, subject to applicable legal exceptions. You may complain to the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP). Other jurisdictions may provide additional rights and deadlines.

You can stop using a channel, disconnect an optional integration or request deletion without an active Nuntiuz login. Disconnecting, logging out, revoking a permission, or deleting your local browser history does not by itself delete stored conversations. A request concerning a business’s customer records may require that business’s involvement; we will explain who is handling it.

Nuntiuz is intended for business operators and authorized testers, and is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child’s personal information has been provided inappropriately.

International processing

The operator is based in Argentina, but the server, messaging services, AI providers and enabled integrations may process information in other countries. The operator’s country is not a guarantee of server location or exclusive local processing. Contact us for information about the current deployment and providers.

Cross-border processing must have a basis permitted by applicable law. Where a transfer requires additional safeguards, those arrangements must be established before the relevant processing. This policy is not a claim that a particular transfer agreement, certification or regional hosting restriction already exists.

This policy website

This static legal website does not contain a chat widget, account login, analytics script, tracking pixel, or external font request. Its links can open your email application or take you to another provider’s website. Privacy requests sent by email are processed through the operator’s email provider.

When this site is published on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives technical requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s Pages documentation and privacy statement. Hosting the policy there does not host or expose the Nuntiuz application databases.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact Matias Luna, Argentina, at lighttextco@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, authorization codes, access tokens or complete identity documents in your initial email.

We update this policy when the service or its data handling changes and publish the revision date above. Where applicable law requires notice or a new consent, we will provide it before making the relevant change. Previous processing remains subject to the commitments and legal requirements that applied to it.
