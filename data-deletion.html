Request data deletion

A clear way to ask for your Nuntiuz information to be removed.

Send a request by email

Email lighttextco@gmail.com with the subject “Nuntiuz data deletion request”. You do not need a Nuntiuz account or an active login to make a request.

Email a deletion request ↗

The link opens your email application; it does not submit a request automatically. If no email application opens, copy the address and write to us using your usual email service.

Include enough detail to find the records

  • The email address associated with your workspace, if you have one.
  • For WhatsApp messages, the phone number you used, including country code, and the business number or business name you contacted.
  • For website chat, the website and approximate conversation date. A conversation identifier is helpful if you have it.
  • Whether you want a particular conversation or document removed, a connected account removed, or your entire workspace and associated data deleted.

Provide only what is necessary to identify the request. Do not include passwords, two-step PINs, access tokens, authorization codes or a complete identity document in the first email. We will ask for reasonable verification if needed.

What you can request

A request can cover your account details, personal information in saved conversations, assistant or channel records, saved connected-account credentials and metadata, or uploaded knowledge associated with you or a workspace you are authorized to manage. We assess the records in the application database, knowledge index and relevant temporary storage.

If you are a business’s customer, you may contact that business directly or contact us with its name. We may need its involvement to identify the relevant records and act on its instructions. You cannot request another person’s or another business’s unrelated information to be removed.

What happens next

The operator reviews requests manually. We verify the requester’s identity and authority where necessary, clarify scope, and remove or correct the relevant information unless an applicable exception requires it to be kept. We then confirm the action taken or explain any refusal, retained information and available next steps.

Where Argentina’s Law No. 25,326 applies, correction, updating and deletion requests must be addressed within five business days of receipt, subject to legal exceptions. Access requests have a 10-calendar-day response period. Other applicable laws may provide different deadlines. Identity verification does not create an indefinite extension of those deadlines.

Legal retention requirements or other people’s protected rights can limit deletion. Backups or snapshots, if present, are assessed as part of the request; we explain any retained copies and how they will be handled. We also assess whether relevant service providers need to be notified.

We cannot erase a copy independently held by the recipient of a message, WhatsApp/Meta, your email provider, or another provider solely by deleting a Nuntiuz record. Where those parties control their own copies, their own request procedures may also be needed.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may contact Argentina’s Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP) or another competent data-protection authority.

Disconnecting is different from deletion

Disconnecting an integration stops Nuntiuz from using the saved connection and removes its locally saved credentials. It does not erase existing conversations or remove records held independently by the provider.

Pausing or removing a channel or assistant can make it unavailable without erasing its database records. Logging out, clearing browser storage or starting a new website chat also does not delete earlier server-side conversations. For complete removal, send the request above.

For a Google connection, you can also manage authorization through your Google account’s connections page. Meta permissions can be managed through the relevant account’s app or business-integration settings. Revoking provider access and deleting Nuntiuz data are separate actions.
